Audit & mobile

One event trail for the door, the camera, and the operator.

Every grant, denial, observation, and manual door command lands in a local audit trail, then syncs to the LAN hub and the iPhone companion.

Access eventsObservationsDoor commandsCSV export

Event types

AccessKiosk verification, granted or denied, with door action when bound.
ObservationCamera saw an enrolled identity; no door action.
Door commandManual unlock or failed command, attributed to the operator.

Search and review

  • Search by identity, employee ID, role, reason, camera, door, or operator.
  • Filters: all, granted, denied, camera observations, door commands.
  • Date ranges: today, 7 days, 30 days, all time.
  • 7-day access-decision chart and summary statistics.
  • CSV export of the filtered set, including door and operator fields.

iPhone companion

The iOS app is read-only. It shows the site audit stream, camera observations, and door commands, caches events in SQLite for offline reads, and requires Face ID or the device passcode before revealing the cache.

Privacy boundary: the phone never receives biometric templates or camera frames. The hub remains the authority for identity and event history.
Audit limits: local and hub events are not yet hash-chained or individually device-signed. Protect the hub database and backups, and restrict operator access to the kiosk.

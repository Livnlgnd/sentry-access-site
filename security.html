Security & limits

“Where does the face data go?” has a short answer.

Nowhere. Templates are encrypted on the kiosk, matching happens locally, and only the resulting decision is synced. Camera gateway observations are limited to already-enrolled identities — unknown passersby are not identified or logged.

Data minimization

No image retention. No raw video upload. Audit events carry the identity, decision, camera or door label, confidence, and time — not biometric payloads.

Credential isolation

RTSP URLs and controller passwords are encrypted through the OS keystore and excluded from IndexedDB, CSV exports, audit payloads, and hub data.

Recoverable by design

The kiosk keeps operating if the hub is offline, queues audit events, and reconciles later. Backups cover the database, keys, and enrollment secret.

What this release does not claim

  • Not a certified access-control or life-safety product. Use fail-safe hardware, independent REX, and fire-alarm release.
  • No REX/door-position supervision, held/forced-door alarm, anti-passback, or two-person rule yet.
  • No automatic ONVIF discovery and no Ring support. Cameras and NVRs use manual RTSP/HTTP URLs.
  • Liveness is a basic challenge-response gate, not certified presentation-attack detection.
  • Manual unlock is available for commissioning and is attributed to the signed-in operator.
  • The Windows installer is not yet Authenticode-signed; Azure Trusted Signing is pending identity validation and certificate-profile approval.
  • Biometric consent, signage, retention, and DPIA obligations remain with the deploying organization.

Live verification

A local 1:N decision in under a second.

The kiosk captures a live sample, checks liveness and quality, compares it against every enrolled template, and writes the decision to the audit trail. No image leaves the device.

1:N matchingBlink / head-turn livenessAES-GCM templatesDoor release

What happens at the kiosk

  • Face detection with bounding box, landmarks, and a subject lock.
  • Quality gate: exactly one face, exposure, focus, framing, and face size.
  • Liveness challenge: eye blink or head turn, switchable at any time.
  • 1:N comparison against decrypted in-memory templates using Euclidean distance.
  • Configurable threshold from 0.30 to 0.70, default 0.50.
  • Granted or denied result with identity, confidence, distance, and reason.
  • Optional relay pulse after policy passes; automatic re-arm for the next person.

Decision model

TemplateFaceNet 128-dimensional descriptor, encrypted with AES-GCM.
ThresholdEuclidean distance; lower is stricter. Default 0.50.
ConfidenceDerived from distance and shown with the decision.
LivenessLocal blink or head-turn challenge, not certified PAD.
AuditDecision, score, liveness method, quality, and operator attribution.
HardwareWindows 10/11 x64 kiosk with a webcam and stable power.
Operational limits: lighting and camera angle materially affect accuracy. The kiosk processes one subject at a time, and liveness is a basic challenge-response gate, not certified presentation-attack detection.

Why local matters

Templates are encrypted at rest and the master key is protected by the Windows OS keystore. Enrollment photos and video frames are not retained. The only biometric payload stored locally is the encrypted 128-float template.

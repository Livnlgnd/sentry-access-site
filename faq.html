FAQ

The answers your client will ask for.

The questions procurement, IT, and the client’s counsel usually ask before a site visit.

Am I buying the software?

No. You are buying a license to use the software, not the software itself. The license is revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and limited to the licensed site and capacity. Vybelive LLC retains all ownership of the software.

What happens if the terms are violated?

Violating the terms of sale, acceptable-use rules, biometric-privacy law, or payment obligations is a material breach and results in immediate license revocation. The license ID becomes void, the right to use ends, support and updates stop, and the customer must uninstall the software. See the revocation clause.

Does the system need internet access?

No. Verification, door policy, camera processing, audit storage, and the LAN hub all run on site. Internet is only needed if you choose to expose the hub remotely, and that is not part of this release.

Which cameras and NVRs are supported?

Any source that exposes an RTSP, RTSPS, or HTTP MJPEG URL, including NVR channel streams. The bundled FFmpeg handles the decode. Automatic ONVIF discovery and Ring are not included.

Which door hardware does it control?

AXIS A1210/A1601 network door controllers through VAPIX, or a generic HTTP relay endpoint. Wire the relay through a fail-safe strike with independent request-to-exit and fire-alarm release. Hardware is not included.

What happens if the network or controller fails?

The door command fails closed. The kiosk keeps running and queues audit events; it does not fall back to an open state. Site design should still include mechanical egress and local fire release.

Can we white-label it?

Not in this release. Founding integrators get a demo deployment, joint commissioning, and a deployment report you can put in front of your client. Full white-label packaging is evaluated with multi-site agreements.

How is biometric compliance handled?

Sentry Access minimizes data and keeps matching local, but the deploying organization remains responsible for consent, notice, retention, access requests, and any BIPA, GDPR, CCPA, or state-specific obligations. A DPIA and counsel review are required before production use.

How do capacity packs work?

Pro includes 4 doors and 8 camera streams. Add door packs at $350 per door or camera packs at $250 per 4 streams. You receive an updated signed license file; no reinstall or new contract is required.

How do software updates work?

The desktop app checks a signed update feed and installs new builds only while annual support is active. Manifests are ECDSA-signed, installers are SHA-256 checked, and the core keeps working if support lapses. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn.

How do refunds and licensing work?

Starter and Pro are perpetual site licenses delivered electronically. Capacity packs update the signed license file. See the terms for the current policy; contact us before purchase if procurement needs a specific clause.

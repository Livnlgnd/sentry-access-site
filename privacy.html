Privacy policy

This policy explains what Sentry Access collects through this website, how sales inquiries are handled, and where biometric and camera data actually live.

Effective: November 23, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Sentry Access is a product of Vybelive LLC (“Vybelive”, “we”, “us”), the company behind KeyAudit Pro. Vybelive sells local-first biometric access-control software and related deployment services. Our contact for privacy questions is joshua@keyauditpro.com.

We operate as a digital-first business and do not publish a street address. If you need a postal address for a legal notice or a vendor form, email us and we will provide the current mailing address for Vybelive LLC.

2. Scope

This policy covers this marketing website and our sales and support communications. It does not govern the biometric data processed by a customer’s installed Sentry Access deployment. That deployment runs on infrastructure controlled by the customer, who is normally the data controller for its employees, contractors, and visitors.

3. What this website collects

3.1 No accounts, analytics, or advertising cookies

This website does not provide user accounts, run third-party analytics, or set advertising cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses, user-agent strings, and request metadata in standard server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

3.2 Checkout

Starter and Pro licenses, capacity packs, and annual support are sold through Stripe. When you select a purchase option, you are redirected to a Stripe-hosted checkout page. Stripe collects payment, billing, and contact details under its own privacy policy. We receive the information needed to fulfill the order, such as your name, email, company, billing country, and order status. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers.

3.3 Sales and support communications

If you email us or request a deployment call, we collect the information you provide: name, work email, company, role, site and hardware details, and the content of the conversation. We use it to respond, scope the deployment, and maintain a record of the commercial relationship.

3.4 Optional scheduling

If a scheduling link is enabled, the scheduling provider processes your name, email, and meeting details under its own policy. The provider is identified on the scheduling page before you book.

4. Biometric and camera data boundary

Sentry Access is designed so that biometric templates and camera frames remain on the customer’s local network. In a normal deployment:

  • Face templates are encrypted and stored on the kiosk.
  • Matching and liveness checks run locally.
  • Camera frames are decoded in memory and are not retained or uploaded.
  • Camera observations are limited to already-enrolled identities.
  • The optional hub stores identity metadata, audit events, and encrypted templates on the customer’s own infrastructure.

We do not receive biometric templates, raw camera frames, or the customer’s audit database unless the customer deliberately shares a diagnostic export with us. If we are engaged to provide remote support, we may see screen-shared configuration or log excerpts; we ask customers to redact biometric and personal data before sharing.

The deploying organization is responsible for providing notice, obtaining any required consent, setting retention periods, honoring access and deletion requests, and completing any required data protection impact assessment. Biometric data may be “sensitive” or “special category” data under laws such as Illinois BIPA, GDPR, and comparable state statutes.

5. How we use information

  • To respond to sales, technical, and support inquiries.
  • To scope and deliver licenses, capacity packs, and support.
  • To process payments, invoices, refunds, and tax obligations.
  • To maintain business records and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, non-identifying feedback.

6. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

  • Contract: to deliver a license, capacity pack, or support you requested.
  • Legitimate interests: to respond to business inquiries, secure the site, and manage the commercial relationship.
  • Legal obligation: to keep tax, accounting, and compliance records.
  • Consent: where we explicitly ask for it, such as optional marketing follow-up. You can withdraw consent at any time.

7. Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the business, under appropriate contractual terms:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. GitHub may process IP addresses and request metadata in standard server logs under its own privacy statement.
  • Stripe — checkout, payment processing, receipts, and tax calculation where enabled.
  • Email and scheduling providers — sales and support communications, when configured.
  • Professional advisers — legal, accounting, and insurance providers under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism. The customer’s on-site deployment does not transfer biometric data to us by default.

9. Retention

Sales inquiries are retained for as long as needed to evaluate and support the relationship, then archived or deleted according to business and legal requirements. Payment and tax records are retained for the period required by applicable law. Support diagnostics are retained only as long as needed to resolve the issue, unless a longer period is agreed.

10. Security

We use access controls, encrypted storage, and least-privilege service accounts for business systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report a suspected issue to joshua@keyauditpro.com with the subject “Security report”.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal information, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Send requests to joshua@keyauditpro.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records required by law.

California residents may request information about categories of personal information collected and disclosed. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

12. Children

This is a business product and website. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the product, law, or our providers. The effective date at the top will change, and material updates will be posted on this page.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: joshua@keyauditpro.com.
